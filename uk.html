Incense from Bangalore, made by one family since 1922.

Onam Agarbathi makes incense sticks, cones and dhoop under the Vaishak and Onam brands. We formulate and manufacture in-house, supply India’s defence canteens, and export to the Gulf, the Caribbean and the Indian Ocean. We are now looking for a UK trade partner.

A manufacturer with a hundred years behind it.

Our great-grandfather started National Flag Perfumery Works in 1922. The Onam brand was born inside it. The family launched Onam Agarbathi as its own company in 1972, with Vaishak as its first brand.

Perfumers and manufacturers
We blend our own fragrances and make every product at our Bangalore factory.
Three National Packaging Awards
Chaitra folds into a prayer chariot, Zoon into a Kashmiri houseboat, and Thaipusam into a temple. The Chaitra pack was a first in FMCG anywhere in the world.
Supplier to India’s defence canteens
We supply the CSD and CPC canteen networks, which hold their suppliers to strict quality and delivery standards.
Already exporting
Regular shipments to the UAE, Oman, Trinidad and Réunion, under export licence and with full documentation.

How we work with distributors.

Indicative terms to start the conversation. We agree the final terms with each partner before the first order.

Minimum order
A mixed-range order, packed in master cartons. Exact minimum agreed per shipment.
Samples
A sample set of the full range can be sent by courier on request.
Lead time
About 4 to 6 weeks from confirmed order to dispatch.
Payment
Advance on order, with the balance against shipping documents.
Shipping
FOB or CIF from Nhava Sheva (Mumbai). We handle export clearance in India.
UK side
Import clearance, UK VAT and local distribution are handled by the UK partner.
Documents
Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and export paperwork with every shipment.
Exclusivity
Open to discussing UK exclusivity, tied to an agreed annual volume.
