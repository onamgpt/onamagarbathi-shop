Last updated 10 September 2026

The delivery charge is shown in your cart before payment and appears as a separate line on your tax invoice. We ship across India.

Delivery time

Orders are usually dispatched within 1–2 working days. Delivery typically takes 7–10 working days depending on your location. Remote pin codes may take longer.

You will receive an order confirmation on WhatsApp and by email, and a tax invoice once the order is processed.

Returns

If something is wrong with your order, you may request a return within 7 days of delivery.

We accept returns where:

  • the product arrived damaged or broken
  • you received the wrong item
  • the product is defective

Incense is a scented consumable product. For hygiene and safety reasons we cannot accept returns of opened or partly used packs, except where the product is genuinely defective.

Please photograph any damage before opening further. A photo sent to us on WhatsApp is usually all we need to process a replacement or refund quickly.

Refunds

Once a return is approved, the refund is issued to your original payment method within 7 working days. Where the fault is ours — damaged, wrong or defective goods — we refund the delivery charge as well.

Refunds for orders paid through Razorpay are returned to the same card, UPI ID or bank account used for the payment. The time it then takes to appear depends on your bank.

Cancellations

An order can be cancelled any time before it is dispatched, for a full refund. Message us on WhatsApp with your order number as soon as possible. Once a parcel has been handed to the courier it cannot be cancelled, but you can still return it under the terms above.

Damaged or missing parcels

If a parcel arrives damaged, or does not arrive at all, contact us within 7 days of the expected delivery date and we will trace it with the courier and replace or refund the order.
