Fine French Perfumes

Premium incense sticks · Eight fragrances

Each stick burns like a fine perfume, in three phases: opening, heart and base. Made in Bangalore by Onam Agarbathi, a family craft since 1922.

The collection

Eight fragrances.
Fine French perfumes.

Fragrance compounds, natural resins and binding agents are blended into a paste and rolled onto a bamboo core, a centuries-old Bangalore technique. Each stick burns for 45–70 minutes and leaves a base trail that lingers for hours.

Not a reed diffuser, a candle or a perfume oil: a fragrance that changes as it burns and fills a room without overpowering it. Positioned for the premium end of the gift and lifestyle market, alongside room-scent products retailing at £15–£28, with a margin that rewards independent retail.

The heritage behind the collection

Four generations. One hundred years. One craft.

Made by Onam Agarbathi Pvt. Ltd., founded in Bangalore in 1972 as the successor to National Flag Perfumery Works (1922). A century of incense craft, exported to 35+ countries, with national awards for fragrance and packaging innovation. All fragrance development is done in-house in Bangalore.

ZoonThe outer box becomes an intricate Kashmiri houseboat. National Award.
ThaipusamFor Malaysia: the outer box becomes an ornate Tamil temple.
